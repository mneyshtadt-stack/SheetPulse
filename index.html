Historical Contribution looks backward — based on money originally invested, it measures which holdings generated your profits over time.
Current Weighted Contribution looks at today — based on current market value, it measures which holdings currently have the biggest influence on your portfolio's performance.
Analogy: Historical = "who scored the goals during the season?" Current weighted = "who's on the field influencing the game right now?"

Historical contribution to total return — cost-weighted

Each bar is a holding's gain or loss as a share of your total cost basis — these add up exactly to your overall total return.

Current weighted contribution to total return % ?

Each bar is a holding's own total return %, weighted by how large it is in the portfolio today — this reflects today's sizing, not your original cost, so it won't sum to the same total as the chart above.
